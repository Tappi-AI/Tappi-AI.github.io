About

Most work is a table

A backlog is a table. A content calendar is a table. A supply chain is a table. What changes is the shape of the rows and how you need to look at them.

What Tappi gives you

Typed columnsFields that match the data — not everything as text
A doc per rowEvidence attached where it belongs
Multiple viewsTable, kanban, timeline over one dataset
Published pagesAny row can render as a shareable document

The same records serve a planner, a reviewer, and an auditor without three separate tools.

Why an ESG demo

Illustrative

We wanted a showcase with real structure: rows needing traceable identity, an origin and a date, certifications that vary per row, and a supply-chain narrative that's more than one text field. ESG product tracking hits all of it — and it ends in a genuinely useful artifact, a digital product passport aligned to the EU ESPR textile data model.
