# About

Tappi is a workspace built around one idea: most work is a table. A backlog
is a table. A content calendar is a table. A supply chain is a table. What
changes is the shape of the rows and how you want to look at them.

Tappi gives you that shape — columns of the type your data actually needs,
a document attached to every row, and views (table, kanban, timeline) that
let the same data serve a planner, a reviewer, and an auditor without three
separate tools.

## Why an ESG demo

We wanted a showcase with real structure: rows that need traceable identity
(an ISBN), an origin and a date, certifications that vary per row, and a
narrative (the supply-chain path) that's more than a single text field. ESG
product tracking hits all of that, so it's what we built first — see it on
the [ESG Demo]({{ '/esg-demo/' | relative_url }}) page.

## Status

Tappi is under active development. This site tracks the product as it's
built, using the ESG scenario as a running example rather than a finished
case study.
